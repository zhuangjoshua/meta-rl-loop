# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
The creative identity is count-first: adoption-count proof is the earned lead
angle, because at matched spend in the same cells count-led ads consistently
produce the most sign-ups and demo requests per dollar. Run three video ads
per batch: TWO distinct count-led executions (different adoption numbers,
framings, or count surfaces — never the same count ad twice) plus ONE
rotating non-count probe drawn from the other declared angle families:
benefit, outcome, story (named customer). Probe angles are never retired;
this preference rests on sign-up/demo evidence only, and settled
purchase-layer evidence, once it exists, outranks it. No single execution (a
specific ad's concrete surface/framing) may run in more than two consecutive
batches; after two straight runs it must sit out at least one batch before
returning. A demo variant (screen-recording of the product working) is an
available production option. The named-story ad is a proven performer on
settled sign-ups and demos per dollar and is guaranteed one slot in the next
batch.

## Coverage ledger (standing rule)
For every axis this policy owns (angle family, demo variant, objective,
audience, budget mode), track which values have had a funded test. An unpriced
value is UNKNOWN, not neutral: no axis value may be preferred, demoted, or
retired while sibling values of that axis remain unpriced, and before refining
among swept values the batch must service unswept ones at the cheapest viable
dose. Priced so far: all four angle families; the demo variant; clicks,
pageviews, leads, and sales objectives; broad, interest_biztools, and
interest_niche audiences. No axis value is currently unpriced.

## Campaigns
Fixed-budget portfolio only, $200 total per batch, concentrated into exactly
THREE cells so each ad-by-cell pairing is funded deeply enough for goal-layer
evidence to settle. At least two of the three cells must be sales-objective
campaigns; the third cell may be any objective. Within the sales cells,
budget tilts toward the audience with the stronger settled demos-per-dollar
record: the sales cell on interest_niche runs at $90 and the sales cell on
broad runs at $44 (broad stays funded — a tilt is not a retirement), with the
third cell at the remaining $66. Auto-budget mode is removed from the standard portfolio: it
repeatedly failed to produce readable, trusted results at portfolio budget
scale, and it may return only through a future design thesis backed by
replicated readability evidence. Audiences available: broad,
interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. While no settled purchase
evidence exists, settled demos-per-dollar is the DEFAULT criterion for
choosing among cells and creatives — demo requests are the deepest readable
pre-purchase event. The first settled purchase immediately outranks this
default and reclaims priority for purchase-layer evidence.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

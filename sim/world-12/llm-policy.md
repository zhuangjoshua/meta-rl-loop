# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. Briefs default to words-led production (no
screen-recording spine): matched same-angle pairs have repeatedly shown no
advantage for demo footage, so demo is used only when a brief states an
argument for it. No pair is mandated. Declared angle families: benefit,
outcome, count (adoption numbers), story (named customer). Story and outcome
are the default slots in every batch; the third slot goes to count or benefit
only when a brief states an argument for it, and otherwise carries another
story or outcome variant. The default batch is two story variants (distinct
named customers) plus one outcome ad. No angle is retired.

Every brief states the offer plainly — "$29/mo, card-required trial" — as the
default qualification line, so clicks arrive knowing the price and the card
requirement. This is a default, not a settled causal claim.

## Bottleneck metric
Every batch read must report the signup-to-purchase rate per matched sales
cell as the tracked bottleneck metric, and per-cell signup-advancement
(signup to demo or purchase) for every cell including the upper-funnel probe;
the step after the click is the portfolio's suspected constraint until
purchases settle regularly.

## Campaigns
Portfolio per batch, $200 total, fixed-budget mode only. Sales campaigns are
the core: they receive all budget except one $15 upper-funnel probe campaign
(broad audience) whose objective alternates between pageviews and leads from
batch to batch; upper-funnel signups are treated as non-evidence of progress
until they advance. Auto-budget mode is banned pending a future policy
revision. Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

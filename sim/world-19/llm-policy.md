# Meta Ads Policy — v8 (world 19 lineage)

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch, with angles assigned per cell type:
pageviews-broad runs a plain (no-demo) benefit-led ad; sales cells run an
outcome-led ad. All outcome-led ads carry the demo variant (screen-recording
of the product working) wherever they run — the demo is a named part of the
outcome execution. Benefit-led ads run plain. Story (named customer) and
count (adoption numbers) run only as deliberate challengers when a matched
comparison is wanted. Each batch writes fresh copy for its assigned angles —
new expressions of the same angle. An ad prompt is never reused verbatim at
any spacing except as a deliberate matched re-test of creative fatigue.

## Campaigns
Portfolio per batch, $200 total, built from fixed single-audience campaigns
only. Budget follows the proven pairs: the pageviews-broad cell (running the
plain benefit-led ad) and the sales-broad cell (running the outcome-led ad)
take the two largest cell budgets. Remaining budget may fund other fixed
cells — leads and interest audiences (interest_biztools, interest_niche) —
for goal-layer confirmation and matched comparisons. No auto-mode campaigns:
budget goes only to fixed single-audience cells until a mechanism exists to
make auto-mode receipts settle as trusted.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

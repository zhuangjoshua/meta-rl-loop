DOSES:

3. Soft rule: each batch includes >=1 demo variant; note the demo/outcome confound and that a matched same-angle pair (±demo) must resolve it.
4. Default: >=1 demo variant every batch AND the next batch must carry the matched same-angle ±demo pair.
5. Demo-forward default: 2 of 3 ads carry demo until the matched pair settles the confound; non-demo kept as the control.

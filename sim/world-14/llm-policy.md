# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. Demo (screen-recording of the product working)
is a per-ad writer's choice, not a default: same-angle with/without-demo
matched pairs at equal spend have measured the format as performance-neutral,
so choose it only where it fits the ad being made. All ads are
named-customer-story-led by default: named-story leads have beaten every
other angle family tested in one-difference matched-cell comparisons (CTR,
per-dollar sign-ups, and the portfolio's goal events). A non-story angle runs
only as an explicit funded test, at most one ad slot per batch. Declared
angle families: benefit, outcome, count (adoption numbers), story (named
customer). Story ads also carry a declared story-subject vertical; the
vocabulary is: founder/agency (a founder whose client intake and follow-up
runs itself), healthcare-ops, legal-office, other-SMB. Founder/agency is the
default vertical for every story slot — it has won fresh-script matched
vertical pairs in two of three matched eras, including the goal events —
EXCEPT when the coverage ledger owes another vertical a funded test, in
which case exactly one slot runs that vertical (fresh script, matched
against a founder slot where possible).
Angle families, story verticals, and audiences that have never had a
funded test are UNKNOWN, not bad; fund unswept values before refining among
swept ones, and no family or vertical is ever blacklisted.

## Campaigns
Broad-audience portfolio per batch, $200 total: a pageviews campaign
(broad), a leads campaign (broad), and sales campaigns on broad. Broad
cells have produced nearly all settled purchases per dollar; premium-CPM
interest-targeted cells underdelivered at the goal layer across sustained
funded spend. Interest audiences (interest_biztools, interest_niche) are
not blacklisted: every third batch, exactly one interest audience runs as
a single minimum-viable probe cell ($20-30, sales objective, fresh
creative), and a probe that wins on revenue per dollar against matched
broad spend re-opens the audience. An auto-budget sales campaign remains
optional; auto cells that stay unreadable two batches running are cut.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. Judge cells per-dollar,
never raw counts; compare conversion rates only within matched cells (same
objective, same audience).

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

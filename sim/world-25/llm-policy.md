# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. Declared angle families: benefit, outcome,
count (adoption numbers), story (named customer). A demo variant (screen-
recording of the product working) is an available production option.

Sweep-first rule: any creative value that has never had a funded test (an
unpriced value) must be funded before any already-tested family appears in a
second repeat batch. All four angle families and the demo variant now have
funded tests.

Slot shape (standing default): two of the three slots run distinct
named-customer story variants, and the two variants must span two different
customer verticals in every batch. Story prompts follow the codified
pattern: a named customer, a named vertical, and one concrete quantified
result. The third slot is a rotating comparator drawn from outcome, count,
and benefit, taking the least-recently-priced family first. Slot weighting
follows replicated matched-cell evidence and is not a retirement: every
family keeps returning to funding through the rotation.

No angle family may be declared preferred, demoted, or retired until its
funded spend would expect at least three purchases at the portfolio's
observed purchase rate.

Coverage ledger (kept current in this policy): angles — benefit, outcome,
count, story all priced; demo variant priced; objectives — pageviews, leads,
sales, clicks all priced; audiences — broad, interest_biztools,
interest_niche all priced; budget modes — fixed priced, auto retired
unreadable.

## Campaigns
Fixed-mode readable cells only, $200 total per batch, sales-weighted by
default. The portfolio shape is FROZEN until the accumulation bar below is
met: sales $140 split near-equal thirds — broad $46, interest_biztools $47,
interest_niche $47 (the niche cell doubles as the experiment/revenue-probe
cell) — plus a pageviews campaign (broad) $30 and a leads campaign (broad)
$30. Purchases are the only verdict currency; sign-up counts are leans,
never verdicts.

Accumulation bar: no audience arm's budget share may change until that arm's
accumulated matched-cell spend would expect at least 3 purchases at the
portfolio's observed purchase rate. Below-bar purchase reads are recorded as
leans and may never move budget; purchases are too rare for single-era
counts to steer allocation.
Auto-budget mode is removed from the standard portfolio: it produced only
unreadable windows and may not return without a readability fix.
Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. Judge cells per-dollar, never
raw counts; never compare conversion rates across differently-targeted cells.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

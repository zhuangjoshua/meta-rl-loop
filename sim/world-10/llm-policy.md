# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. Every ad is a named-customer story WITH demo
footage: a real, named person narrating their use of the product over
screen-recording of the product actually working. Plain (no-demo) story
variants leave the default rotation; revisit them only if the demo ads'
purchase rate collapses. The other declared angle families — benefit, outcome,
count (adoption numbers) — may appear only as supporting lines inside a story,
never as the lead angle. Prefer including at least one never-run persona in
each batch: reran personas have shown decay by their third-to-fourth
consecutive era.

## Campaigns
Portfolio per batch, $200 total, fixed-mode cells only: auto-budget mode is
dropped from the standard portfolio; its budget goes to fixed sales cells.
Every fixed cell must be sized at least $30 so all cells clear the
readability bar. Compose from: a pageviews campaign (broad), a leads campaign
(broad), and sales campaigns. Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools,
interest_niche. At least 50% of the total batch budget runs in broad-audience
cells; interest-audience cells stay funded at readable size as the standing
per-dollar check (broad cells have produced sign-ups at roughly half the cost
of interest cells; this is a per-dollar direction, not a retirement). The
broad sales campaign is the single largest cell in every batch, at roughly
35% of the total budget: broad sales cells hold the portfolio's best
per-dollar purchase rate, and the other cells continue as funded checks.
The broad sales and broad leads campaigns together take the majority of the
batch budget (~55%): they are the portfolio's only purchase-producing cells
to date, while every other cell remains a funded readable check.

## Coverage ledger (standing)
Every policy-owned axis keeps a coverage ledger: which declared values have had
a funded readable test, which have not. An unpriced value is UNKNOWN, not
neutral. No axis may be declared settled, and no value retired or ranked
against its siblings, while any value of that axis remains unpriced. Any
newly-unpriced value must be priced at cheapest viable dose before further
refinement among already-priced values.
Current ledger:

- creative format: plain video PRICED, demo variant PRICED
- audience: broad PRICED, interest_biztools PRICED, interest_niche PRICED
- budget mode: fixed PRICED; auto produced only unreadable cells and is dropped

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

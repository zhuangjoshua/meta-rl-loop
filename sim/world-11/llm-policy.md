# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. The anchor angle family is the lived
before/after outcome: every ad opens on the customer's felt change — the
concrete, experienced difference in their working day. Canonical hook
patterns for anchors: (a) time reclaimed — "hours of form admin becomes
minutes"; (b) loss ended — "submissions stop going missing"; (c) drudgery
removed — "nobody rebuilds the same intake form again". Money/ROI arithmetic
framing (dollar math, cost-per-hour claims) is banned from all slots: it
measurably failed. Hook rankings have not been stable era to era, so each
batch rotates across at least two of the canonical hooks with FRESH renderings
— write new creative each batch; reusing an exact prior ad is discouraged, and
last era's winning hook earns no extra slots by winning once.
Declared angle families: benefit, outcome, count (adoption
numbers), story (named customer). Count is sweep-only status: it may appear
only in a designated coverage-sweep slot, never as an anchor choice. One slot
per batch may be a coverage-sweep slot used to price an angle family, format
variant, or audience that has never had a funded test. A demo variant
(screen-recording of the product working) is an available production option;
demo vs no-demo has shown no sign-up-layer difference so far, so use it when
the hook benefits from real product footage.

## Campaigns
Every campaign runs in fixed budget mode; auto-budget mode is not used —
every dollar must land in a cell that produces a trusted, readable receipt.
$200 total per batch, all of it in sales campaigns: pageviews and leads
probes are dropped while the purchase layer is unreadable. Sign-up-efficiency
reweighting is suspended: with the goal layer producing nothing, marginal
sign-up-per-dollar differences between audiences are noise, and chasing them
is not learning. The portfolio is locked to the replicable baseline —
sales/broad $75, sales/interest_biztools $85, sales/interest_niche $40, all
fixed mode — so every batch is a clean, comparable purchase detector. Any
settled purchase unlocks the portfolio and reopens efficiency optimization.

## Purchase-drought ledger
The policy tracks cumulative sign-ups since the last settled purchase. The
60-sign-up tripwire has FIRED and the drought has since deepened past 80
sign-ups with zero purchases ever recorded, confirming a purchase rate below
roughly 4%. While the drought holds, all sign-up-layer creative and audience
conclusions in this policy are provisional and frozen — they are held as
written, not extended, and new sign-up-layer evidence alone does not justify
strengthening them. The
destination/offer — what the ads point at — is therefore the presumptive
binding constraint on ROAS; fixing it is the loop's standing escalation, and
ad-side conclusions are held at the sign-up layer meanwhile. Any settled
purchase resets the ledger, lifts the portfolio lock, and reopens normal
ROAS judgment.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. Judge cells per dollar, never
raw counts, and compare conversion behavior only within matched cells (same
objective, same audience). Sign-up-layer evidence does not settle goal-layer
(purchase) questions.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

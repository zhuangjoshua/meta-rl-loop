objectives, but the count angle family and the interest_niche audience have never
had a funded test — they are UNKNOWN, not neutral. With only 1 settled purchase on
$200 no swept value can honestly be ranked, so the single supported change of
direction is: the policy must service its unpriced axis values (count angle,
interest_niche audience) with funded tests before any refinement among swept ones.
Falsified if: after those funded tests, coverage-mandated cells were unreadable or
the sweep displaced no decision (i.e. priced values already dominated at matched
cells).

DOSES:
1. Add one sentence: the next batch must include one count-angle ad.
2. Next batch must include one count-angle ad AND one interest_niche sales cell.
3. Coverage lean: until every enumerated angle/audience value is priced, each batch reserves one ad slot and one campaign slot for unpriced values; add a coverage ledger section.
4. Coverage default: each batch funds every still-unpriced angle family and audience before repeating priced ones; ledger tracked in the policy; no axis may be called settled while values are unpriced.
5. Coverage rule: ledger in policy; refining or retiring any axis value forbidden until its whole axis is priced; >= 2 ad slots and >= $80/batch go to unpriced values until sweep completes.
6. Rewrite the policy core coverage-first: the experiment plan IS the sweep — next batches enumerate and fund ALL unpriced values (count, interest_niche, clicks objective) at cheapest viable dose, refinement forbidden until the ledger shows every axis fully priced.

DRAW: dose 5 -> ADOPTED as v1. Policy now carries a binding coverage ledger:
no refinement/retirement while an axis has unpriced values; >=2 ad slots and
>=$80/batch fund unpriced values (count angle, interest_niche audience, clicks
objective) until the sweep completes.

# Iteration 2 (policy v1, seed 2002)

THESIS (pair class): At matched cells (same objective+audience, equal spend,
five cells), the demo screen-recording variant of the count angle caused more
and cheaper sign-ups than the identical non-demo count ad (5 sign-ups + 1
product-demo request vs 2 + 1 on $66.65 each), so the policy should shift
creative production toward demo variants. Signup-layer evidence only — it does
not demote the story angle, which still holds the portfolio's only purchase.
Falsified if: future matched demo/non-demo pairs show no sign-up advantage, or
demo-heavy batches lower settled purchases per dollar.

DOSES:
1. One sentence: prefer the demo variant when producing a count-angle ad.
2. Lean: demo preferred for any angle unless the format needs direct-to-camera (story).
3. Default: at least 2 of 3 ads per batch are demo variants.
4. Strong default: demo for all ads unless an explicit test rationale; keep one non-demo slot while story remains the only purchase source.
5. Rule: every non-story ad must be a demo variant; each batch keeps one matched demo/non-demo pair to keep the effect measured.
6. Core rewrite: demo-first creative identity — every ad in every batch is a demo variant (story angles told over screen-recording too); non-demo production retired pending a future funded test.

DRAW: dose 4 -> ADOPTED as v2. Demo is now the default production format for
all ads (non-demo needs an explicit test rationale); one non-demo slot stays
reserved for story(named) while it remains the only purchase source. Ledger
updated: all axes priced except auto mode (unreadable once).

# Iteration 4 (policy v3, seed 2004)

THESIS (pattern class, 4 eras / $800): The portfolio reliably produces sign-ups
in every era (19 at roughly $42 each) but purchases essentially never settle
(1 purchase, era 1) — the binding constraint is conversion after the sign-up,
not creative reach — so spend and creative should tilt toward purchase intent
(sales-objective cells, offer-forward creative) rather than more sign-up
volume. Confirming instrumentation (required for this class): a standing
per-cell ledger of sign-up -> demo -> purchase each era; the thesis is
confirmed if purchase-tilted cells settle purchases at better per-dollar rates,
falsified if purchases stay flat while sign-up volume drops.
(Secondary, logged not adopted: matched-cell pair it4-outcome-demo beat
it4-count-demo 4-1 on sign-ups at equal spend.)

DOSES:
1. Instrumentation only: add a standing-items ledger tracking sign-up -> demo -> purchase per cell per era.
2. Ledger + lean: prefer sales-objective cells when composing the portfolio.
3. Default: at least 60% of budget to sales-objective cells; ads carry an offer-forward purchase CTA; ledger mandatory.
4. Reallocation: drop the pageviews cell (its sign-ups have never purchased), move its $40 to sales cells; offer-forward creative is the default; ledger mandatory.
5. Purchase-first default: sales cells hold at least $120; one leads cell kept as a sign-up control; every ad's CTA sells the $29 purchase directly; ledger mandatory.
6. Max pattern dose: portfolio recomposed around the purchase step — sales-objective cells own at least 70% of budget, pageviews parked in the ledger (revivable), every creative brief leads with the offer, and the per-cell funnel ledger auto-falsifies the thesis if purchases do not improve within two funded eras.

DRAW: dose 5 -> ADOPTED as v4. Purchase-first allocation (sales cells >= $120,
one leads-broad sign-up control), every ad CTA sells the $29 purchase
directly, and a binding per-cell funnel ledger (sign-up -> demo -> purchase)
with an explicit falsification condition.

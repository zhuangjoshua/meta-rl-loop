# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch, all plain named-customer story: every ad leads
with a real named customer's story, told without demo footage. Demo
(screen-recording) is removed from the declared production-variant vocabulary:
paired matched-cell tests showed no advantage over plain story. Coverage-test
slots exist to price untested options; each batch may designate slots to fund
any angle family that has never had a funded test. Declared angle families:
benefit, outcome, count (adoption numbers), story (named customer).

Declared CTA-intent axis: trial-intent (invitation to try/start) and
purchase-intent (price-forward, buy-now framing). Each batch runs one
purchase-intent slot and one trial-intent control slot so the two framings
can be compared in matched cells on purchases and signup quality per dollar;
the third slot alternates between the two intent values from batch to batch
so cumulative comparison spend stays near 1:1. The comparison has shown no
purchase separation after two balanced eras, so trial-intent is the working
default wherever a framing choice is otherwise unconstrained while the
comparison continues.

## Campaigns
Standard portfolio per batch, $200 total, all cells fixed-budget,
sales-majority as standing structure: purchases are the goal event, and the
only purchase to date came from purchase-optimized spend, so sales campaigns
carry $190 split across three distinct audiences (about $63 each), while
pageviews (broad, $5) and leads (broad, $5) remain as minimal probes. The
read that matters each batch: purchases per dollar in sales cells versus
probes — the structure stands unless probes ever out-produce sales cells per
dollar on purchases. Auto-budget (platform-allocated) cells are
excluded from the portfolio and may not be re-added without a policy
revision: they have not produced trusted reads at this portfolio's budget
scale. Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch. Every ad, in any angle family, must satisfy
the concreteness checklist: a specific workplace vertical, the artifact
Formflow replaced, a quantified scale count, and a timeframe; a named person
is additionally required only when the angle is story. No angle family holds
a reserved slot. Slots are allocated on merit: rank candidate ads by trailing
per-dollar performance blending settled purchases per dollar (primary) and
sign-ups per dollar (secondary), and fill the three slots with the best
available concrete ads regardless of angle family; ads in one batch should
span at least two different workplace verticals. The top-merit ad each batch
ships as a demo variant (its copy intercut with a screen-recording of
Formflow working), and a second slot carries the identical non-demo twin as
the pair control so the demo effect stays readable at the goal layer. Whenever any declared axis
value is still unpriced (never funded), a sweep obligation is pending and
claims one slot first. Declared angle families: benefit, outcome, count
(adoption numbers), story (named customer). A demo variant (screen-recording
of the product working) is an available production option. Pricing is
goal-layer: an axis value whose only funded readings predate goal-readable
cells (cells dense enough for purchases to settle) counts as UNPRICED, and
the sweep obligation claims one slot per era until it has a goal-layer
reading — the demo variant is first in that queue. Unpriced values are
UNKNOWN, not bad: no angle family, format, or audience may be retired while
it lacks a goal-layer funded test.

## Campaigns
$200 total per batch, fully concentrated so the goal event gets a readable
sample: run ONE sales-objective campaign per batch carrying the entire $200,
with the audience chosen by settled cost per purchase across prior dense
eras. Any audience that has never had a dense-era goal-layer reading gets one
dense era before the audience choice is considered settled; cost per sign-up
may not decide the audience. This is a structural concentration to make
purchases observable, reversible and explicitly not a verdict on non-sales
objectives or other audiences. Each era must report demo->purchase conversion
and conversion lag as a standing item. If two consecutive full-concentration
eras settle zero purchases while cumulative demos reach 10 or more, the
destination/offer is formally implicated and a destination/offer change
experiment becomes a standing design item. Audiences available: broad,
interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. Judge cells per dollar, and
compare conversion rates only within matched cells (same objective, same
audience). Sign-up evidence supports leans only; purchases are the goal
event and goal-layer conclusions require purchase evidence.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

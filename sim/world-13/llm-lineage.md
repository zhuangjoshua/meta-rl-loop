# Iteration 7 (policy v6, seed 1307)

THESIS (pattern, middle doses max): Repeating identical creative executions fatigues them — the exact same story-demo ad ran in it5/it6/it7 and its CPL rose monotonically in both matched audiences (broad $10 -> $40 -> no signups on $50; niche $11.67 -> $25 -> $20), and count-demo x broad collapsed from $4.44 CPL to zero signups one era after its peak — so the policy should mandate fresh creative executions per batch. Direction, not causation (market drift is a confounder). Confirming experiment: iteration 8 runs fresh executions of the same wrappers; recovery of CPL toward early-era levels confirms, no recovery refutes.

DOSES:
1. Add one sentence: identical executions show rising CPL across eras; prefer fresh variants each batch.
2. No creative execution may run more than 2 consecutive batches unchanged.
3. Each batch must introduce at least one brand-new execution (new script/customer/footage).
4. Every ad in every batch must be a fresh execution — wrappers may repeat, scripts/customers/footage must change from the prior batch.
5. All ads fresh every batch, and at least one ad must rotate to a wrapper not used in the prior batch.
6. Creative section rewritten around a rotation pool: executions retire permanently after any era of worsening CPL, and no execution ever appears in 3 batches.

DRAW: dose 2 -> ADOPTED as v7: no creative execution may run more than 2 consecutive batches unchanged; refresh required after 2.
STANDING ITEMS: (1) it5-7 executions are all at/past the 2-batch limit — it8 must run fresh executions (also the thesis's confirming experiment). (2) audience ledger: niche trusted buys 2, broad 1, biztools 0; era winner keeps flipping. (3) biztools demotion threshold-blocked; auto cut-license parked.

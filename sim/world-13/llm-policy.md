# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run two video ads per batch (concentration: every ad-by-cell subcell must
carry enough spend for a readable result). Every ad is built around screen-recording demo
footage of the product working; demo footage is the mandatory production core
of every slot. Declared angle families — benefit, outcome, count (adoption
numbers), story (named customer) — are rendered as voiceover/framing wrappers
on the demo video. Story-first (a named customer) is the default wrapper for
every slot EXCEPT the reserved sweep slot: each batch reserves at least one
slot for an angle family or production option that has never had a funded
test, until every declared option has been priced. Every ad in every batch
must be a fresh creative execution (new script, customer, and footage; the
wrapper family may repeat). If an audience produced zero sign-ups in the
prior batch, its ads must additionally rotate to a different wrapper family
in the next batch.

## Campaigns
$200 total per batch, at most 3 sales cells, and no campaign cell below $50
except the control. All budget goes to sales-objective campaigns EXCEPT one
minimum-viable $20 non-sales control cell (pageviews or leads, rotating),
which must run every batch and may not be retired while its cumulative funded
spend still expects fewer than 3 purchases at the portfolio's observed
purchase rate. An auto-budget sales campaign (platform allocates between
audiences) remains optional. Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools,
interest_niche. The interest_niche audience receives at least 40% of the
sales-objective budget each batch, and the broad audience must be funded with
a sales cell every batch; the remainder is split among the audiences subject
to the $50 cell minimum.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

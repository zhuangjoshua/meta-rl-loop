# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Run three video ads per batch, built around benefit-led product demos: an ad
leads with the concrete benefit (time saved, busywork removed) over
screen-recording footage of the product working. All three slots are
benefit×demo variants by default, and the slots have fixed roles: a LEAD slot
running the hook with the best cumulative purchase-per-dollar across the most
eras (currently "Get your Tuesdays back"); a CHALLENGER slot running the
next-best incumbent hook; and a FRESH-ROTATION slot running a hook variant.
Vary the hook, scene, and phrasing across slots, not the angle. One exploration slot may be explicitly claimed per batch to test a
different angle family or format; the claim must name what the slot is
pricing. Declared creative vocabulary (for exploration bookkeeping): angle
families {benefit, outcome, count, story (named customer)} crossed with demo
{on, off} — every cell of that ledger is priced; benefit×demo holds the
goal-layer evidence (purchases), all other angles hold none. No angle is
retired; non-benefit angles simply require the exploration slot.

## Campaigns
Standard portfolio per batch, $200 total, fixed-mode cells only, over the
cells {sales broad, leads broad, pageviews broad, sales interest_biztools,
sales interest_niche}. No cell holds a hardcoded engine or anchor share:
each batch, every cell's budget is proportional to its lifetime settled
purchases per dollar on the scoreboard, subject to a $15 probe floor for
every cell (a cell with no purchases yet runs at its floor). On current
scoreboard evidence this puts leads (broad) first (~$75) and sales (broad)
second (~$55), with the purchase-free cells at floors. The auto-budget sales campaign is removed from the portfolio — it
never produced a trusted attribution window. No cell is retired; probes keep
every cell priced. Audiences available: broad, interest_biztools,
interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. All creative-hook and budget
verdicts are made from one instrument: the purchases-only scoreboard —
cumulative settled purchases per dollar, tabulated by hook and by cell. That
scoreboard is the only ranking input; signup counts, CPL, and demo counts are
recorded as diagnostics and carry no decision weight, because signup volume
has not tracked purchases. Comparisons are made per-dollar and, for creative,
within matched cells (same objective, same audience) only.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

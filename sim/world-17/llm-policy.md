# Meta Ads Policy — v8

GOAL this policy serves: maximize settled ROAS (revenue / spend) for a $29/mo
product ("Formflow"); purchases are the goal event.

## Creative
Declared angle families: benefit, outcome, count (adoption numbers), story
(named customer). A demo variant (screen-recording of the product working) is
an available production option.

Batches run two ads, not three: the outcome-led house ad plus exactly one
same-cell challenger, so each ad-cell is funded well enough for goal events
to concentrate and settle. Every third batch runs a single house ad all-in,
with no challenger, so the anchor configuration accumulates goal events at
maximum rate. The challenger slot rotates across batches through benefit,
demo-variant, story, and count, so every angle family and production option
keeps getting priced on rotation and none is ever retired. Count, having
lost every matched cell in consecutive funded pricings, is re-priced only
every other rotation cycle; the slots it skips go to the stronger families. Challengers always
share the house ad's cells so comparisons stay one-difference. Any angle
family or production option that has never had a funded test is unknown, not
bad, and is owed a first funded test before its family can be judged.

## Campaigns
Portfolio per batch, $200 total, fixed-mode cells only. Auto-budget mode is
retired: it repeatedly produced untrusted, unreadable windows and bought no
evidence; it may return only through a future design revision that includes a
concrete plan for making its windows readable. The anchor cell is a $150
leads campaign on the broad audience — the configuration where goal events
have actually occurred. The sales objective is kept under surveillance, not
retired: two sales probes run at roughly $25 each, their audiences rotating
across batches through broad, interest_biztools, and interest_niche, so every
audience keeps producing settled receipts on a cycle and any sales-cell goal
event would be seen. Audiences available: broad,
interest_biztools, interest_niche.

## Judgment
Judge only settled results. An ad/cell with no measurement is unknown, not
bad. Never repeat an approach that measurably failed. Unreadable cells
(starved/short-window) are evidence of nothing. Judge cells per dollar, never
by raw counts, and compare only within matched cells (same objective, same
audience). Sign-up-layer evidence may steer creative leans but does not settle
goal-layer (purchase) questions.

## Process
After each batch, the semantic gradient reads all receipts and revises this
policy; a noise schedule picks the revision dose. Every batch's full receipts
accumulate as evidence.

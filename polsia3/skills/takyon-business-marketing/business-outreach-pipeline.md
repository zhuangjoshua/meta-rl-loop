# Takyon Business Skill: Outreach Pipeline

Source material copied and adapted from OpenClaw cold-email, email sequence, revops, sales enablement, lead magnets, competitor alternatives, and BlueCraft-style sequence structure. BlueCraft/MachFive API execution is excluded from this Takyon-owned skill.

Purpose: create an inspectable, no-sending outbound and sales pipeline plan.

Rules:
- Do not send email, DM, comment, post, call, scrape private contact data, or enrich leads through a vendor.
- Use only leads, community targets, documents, memory, and receipts already visible in Takyon.
- Never invent email addresses, names, employers, intent, or replies.
- Prefer small targeted batches and explicit approval before any future sending integration.

Return Markdown with these sections:
- `# Business Outreach Pipeline`
- `## Target Segments`: who to contact and why, grounded in visible leads or communities.
- `## Qualification Rules`: fit, urgency, disqualifiers, and data needed.
- `## Sequence Draft`: 3 to 4 concise touches with subject, body, CTA, and personalization fields.
- `## Sales Enablement`: one-pager bullets, objections, proof, demo angle, follow-up asset.
- `## RevOps Notes`: statuses, owner actions, next receipt needed, and what not to automate.
- `## Vendor Adapter Notes`: optional future keys or providers, marked blocked unless configured.

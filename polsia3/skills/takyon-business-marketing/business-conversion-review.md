# Takyon Business Skill: Conversion Review

Source material copied and adapted from OpenClaw page CRO, signup-flow CRO, onboarding CRO, form CRO, popup CRO, paywall/upgrade CRO, pricing, and A/B test setup skills.

Purpose: identify the smallest website or product changes likely to improve conversion.

Rules:
- Use visible Takyon evidence only: site config, deployment receipts, product docs, checkout rows, revenue rows, jobs, memory, and workspace files.
- Do not claim analytics, sessions, heatmaps, signups, or revenue unless receipts exist.
- Do not edit generated-app files directly. Recommend bounded workflows.
- Prefer a few high-leverage experiments over a long generic CRO checklist.

Return Markdown with these sections:
- `# Business Conversion Review`
- `## Funnel Snapshot`: current visible path from visitor to paid user, with unknowns.
- `## Primary Friction`: the most likely blockers from evidence.
- `## Offer And Pricing Review`: clarity, perceived risk, urgency, trust, and checkout fit.
- `## Page-Level Changes`: headline, proof, CTA, sections, FAQ, comparison, form, paywall, onboarding.
- `## Experiments`: 3 to 5 tests with hypothesis, change, success metric, and required evidence.
- `## Product/UI Candidates`: changes that should route to `website_build_deploy` or `product_ui`.
- `## Measurement Needs`: events or receipts needed before claiming lift.

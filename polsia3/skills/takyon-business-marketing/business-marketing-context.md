# Takyon Business Skill: Marketing Context

Source material copied and adapted from OpenClaw `product-marketing-context`, positioning, pricing, launch, competitor-alternatives, and sales-enablement patterns.

Purpose: create the shared business marketing context every other business skill should read.

Rules:
- Use only supplied Takyon business state, documents, memory, jobs, receipts, and workspace files.
- Do not invent customers, revenue, testimonials, metrics, citations, competitor claims, or vendor receipts.
- If a fact is missing, write it under Unknowns instead of guessing.
- Do not call external APIs, send messages, publish posts, scrape private contacts, or mutate vendor accounts.
- Keep the output useful as a durable file the CEO can inspect later.

Return Markdown with these sections:
- `# Business Marketing Context`
- `## Positioning Kernel`: one-liner, buyer, pain, offer, promised outcome.
- `## ICP`: specific buyer/user wedge, disqualifiers, urgency signals.
- `## Proof And Evidence`: only facts visible in the Takyon context.
- `## Competitive Angle`: alternatives and why this wedge can win.
- `## Channel Fit`: best owned, community, search, social, sales, and paid channels from evidence.
- `## Objections`: likely objections and honest responses.
- `## Messaging Rules`: claims to make, claims to avoid, voice, banned hype.
- `## Unknowns`: missing evidence or files the CEO should seek.
- `## Next Business Skill Inputs`: concise bullets other skills should reuse.

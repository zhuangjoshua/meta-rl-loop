# Outreach Add-On

## Port From V2/Polsia

- Candidate lead storage.
- Hunter optional discovery/verification.
- Company email identity.
- Cold outreach events.
- Rate limits:
  - 2 cold emails/day
  - 20 known-contact emails/day
  - unlimited inbound replies

## UI Rule

Show real leads/targets and real generated outreach copy. Do not show fake leads.

## Sending

Sending can be policy-controlled later.

Default should be conservative unless explicitly configured.

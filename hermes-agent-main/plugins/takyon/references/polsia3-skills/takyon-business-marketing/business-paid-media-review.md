# Takyon Business Skill: Paid Media Review

Source material copied and adapted from OpenClaw paid ads/ad creative skills and Meta Ads Kit read-only monitoring patterns: overview, campaigns, top creatives, bleeders, winners, fatigue, budget recommendations, and copy generation. Meta upload, launch, pause, budget changes, and spend mutation are excluded.

Purpose: produce a paid-media readiness and read-only review that can guide creative, not mutate ad accounts.

Rules:
- Do not create campaigns, upload creatives, launch ads, pause/resume ads, change budgets, or spend money.
- Do not claim Meta performance unless Takyon context includes a real report or receipt.
- If Meta credentials exist but no read-only report exists, say credentials are present but performance evidence is absent.
- Keep all recommendations as drafts or next Takyon workflow candidates.

Return Markdown with these sections:
- `# Business Paid Media Review`
- `## Readiness`: offer, landing page, checkout, measurement, creative assets, risk.
- `## Meta Evidence`: summarize visible Meta/ad rows only. Otherwise mark unknown.
- `## Fatigue And Efficiency Checks`: CTR drop, frequency above 3.5, CPC rise, CPA/ROAS, winners, bleeders, with unknowns when no metrics exist.
- `## Creative Hypotheses`: 3 to 5 angles for Sora/display/social creative.
- `## Copy Variants`: primary text, headline, description, CTA for each angle.
- `## Budget Recommendation`: planning-only recommendation with explicit no-spend status.
- `## Next Workflow Candidates`: usually `meta_seedance`, `business_measurement_plan`, or `business_conversion_review`.

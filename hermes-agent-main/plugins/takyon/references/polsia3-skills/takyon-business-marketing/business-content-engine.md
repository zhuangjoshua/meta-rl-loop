# Takyon Business Skill: Content Engine

Source material copied and adapted from OpenClaw copywriting, copy editing, content strategy, social content, email sequence, lead magnets, free-tool strategy, launch strategy, and competitor-alternatives skills.

Purpose: turn the business marketing context into useful content briefs and drafts.

Rules:
- Use only visible Takyon context and explicit operator direction.
- Do not fabricate case studies, endorsements, market stats, screenshots, search volume, or performance.
- Do not publish posts or send email.
- Keep copy specific to the buyer, pain, offer, proof, and current conversion goal.

Return Markdown with these sections:
- `# Business Content Engine`
- `## Editorial Thesis`: the point of view this business should repeatedly prove.
- `## Content Pillars`: 3 to 5 themes tied to buyer pain and offer.
- `## Page Briefs`: landing, comparison, alternative, use-case, docs, or free-tool pages worth creating.
- `## Social Angles`: short post concepts that can feed `x_social` or a draft-only workflow.
- `## Lead Magnet Or Free Tool`: one concrete asset idea with scope and conversion path.
- `## Draft Copy`: ready-to-use snippets for the highest-priority page or campaign.
- `## Evidence Needed`: what would make the content stronger.

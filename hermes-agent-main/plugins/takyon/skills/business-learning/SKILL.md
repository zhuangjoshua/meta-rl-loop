# Takyon Business Learning

Use this skill when the CEO needs to learn the business, change its mind, or preserve useful judgment for future runs.

The business brain is per-business and freeform under `brain/`. Create whatever files help the CEO operate better:

```text
brain/
  index.md
  strategy.md
  pricing.md
  positioning.md
  product.md
  product_ideas.md
  distribution.md
  customer_segments.md
  objections.md
  failures.md
  decision_journal.md
  open_questions.md
  playbooks.md
  operator_preferences.md
  ceo_notes.md
```

These are suggestions, not a schema. Sharper files are better when the business has a sharper shape.

## Practice

- Keep learning inside the business scope.
- Write beliefs with evidence when available.
- Mark weak beliefs as hypotheses.
- Update strategy, ICP, where users concentrate, pricing, positioning, product ideas, distribution, objections, and playbooks whenever new evidence changes the CEO's mind.
- Keep failures useful: what happened, likely cause, what to avoid, and what to try next.
- Promote lessons from campaign files into `brain/` only when they matter beyond that campaign.
- Do not put per-business facts in `SOUL.md`.

Use `business_record_memory` for durable brain files and `business_record_event` for raw observations.

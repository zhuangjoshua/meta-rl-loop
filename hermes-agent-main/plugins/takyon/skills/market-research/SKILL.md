# Takyon Market Research

Use this skill when the CEO lacks current evidence about customers, competitors, channels, pricing, demand, or language.

## Practice

- Use Takyon web tools when available.
- Keep findings inside the current business or workspace.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Prefer short, decision-useful research notes over bulky dumps.
- Record source URLs, dates, and confidence when possible.
- Treat demand radar as part of market research: capture buyer pain, intent signals, competitor movement, channel concentration, repeated language, and urgency when those signals are missing, stale, or strategically important.
- If canonical market-signal tools exist in `business_registry`, use them for structured demand evidence. Until then, preserve compact signal notes under `research/` or `brain/` and record important observations with `business_record_event`.
- Update the business brain when research changes strategy, pricing, positioning, product, or distribution.

If web tools or provider credentials are unavailable, say what is blocked and commit only local planning work.

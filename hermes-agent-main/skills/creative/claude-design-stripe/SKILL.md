# Claude Design Stripe

Layer this beneath `taste-frontend` and `claude-design` when the product should feel premium,
commercial, precise, and polished. Taste adapts this vocabulary to the brief; do not copy it verbatim.

## When To Use

- fintech
- infra
- commercial B2B
- enterprise marketing
- premium pricing or revenue surfaces

## Visual Direction

- crisp white canvas
- engineered polish
- premium density
- technical but still warm

## Typography

- elegant light-weight display moments
- disciplined sans body
- numerics treated carefully

## Color and Tokens

- deep navy text
- strong brand purple accent
- tasteful magenta/ruby support only when needed
- cool multi-layer shadows

## Components

- compact radii
- polished CTAs
- premium tables, cards, and code/data surfaces

## Hard Rules

- no playful consumer energy
- no over-rounded toy-like controls
- no muddy low-contrast typography
- no generic gradient sludge

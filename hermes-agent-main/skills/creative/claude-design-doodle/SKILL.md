# Claude Design Doodle

Pair this with `claude-design` when the product should feel whimsical, friendly, and intentionally lighthearted.

## When To Use

- pet apps
- dog or cat products
- kid-adjacent or family-friendly tools
- casual social products
- silly or charming consumer SaaS

## Visual Direction

- playful
- informal
- hand-drawn energy
- friendly rather than premium

## Typography

- expressive handwritten or doodled display moments
- keep body text readable and grounded

## Color and Tokens

- bright cheerful primary accent
- strong contrast for readability
- clean surfaces so the playfulness has room

## Components

- friendly buttons and cards
- playful details without sacrificing usability
- clear states and labels

## Hard Rules

- no fake enterprise seriousness
- no muddy muted palette
- no novelty that breaks readability
- no childish mess; playful still has to ship

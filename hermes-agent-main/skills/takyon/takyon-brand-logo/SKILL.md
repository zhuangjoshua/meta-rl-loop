# Brand Logo

Create one distinctive, icon-only mark from established business truth. The business name, category,
audience, tone, and existing visual direction inform the concept; this method does not invent naming,
positioning, or brand voice.

Use it when a business needs a brand logo or icon mark for its product site, app, or social profile,
including when the operator is building or refreshing the brand identity and needs a real
transparent-background asset.

## Method

1. Read the brand brief and existing assets. Identify symbols that are meaningful, ownable, and not
   confused with a category cliché.
2. Define three concept directions in words, then choose the strongest one against distinctiveness,
   relevance, simplicity, and small-size legibility.
3. Write a concise image brief for a flat vector-style icon with transparent background, no text,
   no mockup surface, and no embedded presentation frame.
4. Request the bound logo-generation capability once the concept is resolved.
5. Inspect the real output at large and favicon sizes. Reject accidental lettering, opaque or dirty
   backgrounds, excessive detail, stock-symbol resemblance, and weak silhouette.

## Boundaries

- Performance ad creative belongs to `takyon-static-ad-creative-generator`.
- Generic or artistic image generation, photo editing, and video belong elsewhere.
- Do not add a wordmark, business name, tagline, or invented copy.
- If generation authority is unavailable, report the blocker; do not substitute a placeholder.

## Verification

- The mark is icon-only, transparent, readable at small size, and grounded in the approved brief.
- The silhouette remains recognizable in one color.
- The output is an actual generated asset, not a description or mocked success state.

# X

Own X posts, replies, thread handling, and durable voice for one business. Use the embedded copy
references for hooks, edge, anti-generic editing, and adversarial refinement while grounding every
claim in current business truth.

Use it when the business needs X-native drafting, posting, or reply handling and when a wake or
campaign turn is clearly X-shaped.

## Method

1. Read current business facts, campaign context, recent metrics, durable X voice, and the relevant
   conversation thread.
2. Classify the task: original post, reply, thread continuation, quote response, or conversation
   follow-up.
3. Select one idea and one audience tension. Choose a hook from
   [references/hook-library.md](references/hook-library.md) without forcing a formula that does not fit.
4. Draft in the business's established voice. Prefer concrete observation, earned specificity, and a
   clear point of view over positioning jargon.
5. Apply the edge and voice checks in [references/voice-and-edge.md](references/voice-and-edge.md),
   then remove the AI tells in [references/ai-tells.md](references/ai-tells.md).
6. Run one adversarial refinement pass: challenge the opening, evidence, novelty, rhythm, and reason
   to respond. Preserve any deliberate roughness that makes the voice human.
7. Request the bound X capability only when real publication is intended. Otherwise label the output
   as a draft or suppressed test.
8. For replies, record the sent message and update conversation status only after authoritative send
   evidence. Keep unresolved items unresolved when publishing fails.
9. On later turns, compare actual impressions, replies, bookmarks, clicks, and conversation quality;
   change one material variable at a time.

## Boundaries

- Broad cross-channel campaign coordination belongs to `takyon-distribution`.
- Do not invent quotes, customer results, product behavior, or trend evidence.
- Do not mark a message handled merely because a draft exists.
- Do not claim publication from prose, a local artifact, or a suppressed test.

## Verification

- The copy matches the durable business voice and contains one clear idea.
- Every factual claim is supported by current business evidence.
- Thread and reply context are preserved without duplicating already-sent content.
- Publication and conversation status follow real provider-backed completion evidence.

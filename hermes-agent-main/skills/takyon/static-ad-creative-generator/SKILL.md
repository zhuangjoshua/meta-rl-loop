# Static Ad Creative Generator

Own the creative path from business brief to structured ad specification, compiled art direction,
quality review, and real image render. This method does not launch or manage campaigns.
Use it when the operator wants multiple ad angles as reviewable specifications before or alongside
paid distribution work, or a batch creative test matrix with real generated renders.

## Method

1. Read business truth, audience, offer, proof, brand constraints, platform, placement, and campaign
   objective.
2. Define a test matrix with materially different angles, awareness stages, hooks, visual templates,
   and audience hypotheses. Do not create superficial color-only variants.
3. Write one strict specification per ad: angle, audience, promise, proof, headline, support copy,
   call to action, composition, hierarchy, safe zones, brand treatment, and prohibited claims.
4. Validate the specification against the bundled schema and policy checks.
5. Compile the specification into an art-directed prompt using
   [references/prompt-compiler-rules.md](references/prompt-compiler-rules.md).
6. Request the declared generation capability and inspect the real output.
7. Reject unreadable type, invented product UI, false proof, broken hands or objects, unsafe crops,
   low contrast, accidental marks, and brand drift. Regenerate only with a diagnosed correction.
8. Produce the approved variants and a manifest that preserves each hypothesis and QA result.

## Boundaries

- Generic or artistic image generation, logo and brand-identity design, and video creative belong to
  other methods.
- Long-form landing-page or email copywriting is out of scope.
- Launching, managing, or pulling performance metrics from live ad accounts is out of scope.
- A missing generation capability is a blocker; never substitute a placeholder and call it rendered.

## Verification

- Every variant represents a distinct testable hypothesis.
- Copy and visual proof are grounded in supplied business evidence.
- Specifications validate and respect the target placement's aspect ratio and safe zones.
- Each accepted asset passes the bundled QA rubric and corresponds to its manifest entry.

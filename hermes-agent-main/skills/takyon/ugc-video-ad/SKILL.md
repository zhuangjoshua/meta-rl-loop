# UGC Video Ad

Own per-business UGC video-ad generation from brief to finished video, script, and reference image.
Keep the words and physical production direction separate so neither layer silently rewrites the
other.
The accepted asset is published through the declared artifact binding as a business-scoped creative
deliverable.

## Method

1. Read the product brief: audience, problem, mechanism, proof, objections, desired spokesperson,
   platform, duration, tone, and call to action.
2. Write the dialogue and paired physical action using
   [references/dialogue-action-framework.md](references/dialogue-action-framework.md). Every action
   must be possible for one person in one setting.
3. Define one photoreal reference frame with a credible phone-camera composition, environment,
   wardrobe, expression, and lighting. Avoid polished commercial staging.
4. Divide any sequence longer than the renderer's reliable clip window at natural sentence and
   action boundaries. Preserve identity, wardrobe, setting, camera, lighting, and hand position
   between clips.
5. Request the declared video-generation capability with the structured brief.
6. Join segments at continuity-safe frames, normalize orientation and audio, and apply a restrained
   realism pass. Do not hide generation failures with aggressive effects.
7. Inspect the actual finished media and preserve the script, reference image, and output metadata.

## Boundaries

- Do not use for screen-recording or UI-demo videos, product cutaways, or multi-actor scenes.
- Do not create non-business or cross-business assets.
- Do not read provider keys or call a provider directly; generation authority is external.
- A missing real render is a blocker, not permission to write a fake media record.

## Verification

- The accepted output is vertical, playable, and within the requested duration.
- Spoken dialogue matches the approved script and paired actions.
- Identity and scene continuity hold across every segment.
- The result looks like plausible creator footage rather than a polished studio commercial.

Read [references/realism-framework.md](references/realism-framework.md) and
[references/editing-and-stitching.md](references/editing-and-stitching.md) for detailed production
criteria.

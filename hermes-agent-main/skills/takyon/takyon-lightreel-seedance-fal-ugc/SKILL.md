# Creator Research to UGC Render Payload

Own the company-input to creator-format discovery to Seedance prompt to fal payload method for
single-person UGC video generation. Exact providers, credentials, spend authority, and storage
bindings are supplied outside this skill.

Use it when a team needs a repeatable framework for turning business context into Lightreel research
prompts and fal submission payloads, or when single-person selfie-style constraints must survive
ideation and rendering.

## Method

1. Normalize current company facts into audience, pain, product mechanism, differentiators, proof,
   objections, tone, call to action, and creative constraints.
2. Form a research prompt that asks for a high-performing creator format, hook family, pacing,
   evidence, and platform fit without requesting fabricated performance claims.
3. If the declared discovery capability is unavailable, preserve the prompt and report the blocker;
   do not make a direct paid request.
4. Convert supported findings into one single-person selfie-style concept.
5. Write a Seedance-safe prompt with one speaker, one continuous setting, simple physical action,
   explicit camera behavior, synchronized spoken lines, continuity, and negative constraints.
6. Build a provider-neutral reference-to-video request containing the real reference asset, prompt,
   duration, aspect ratio, audio intent, and output requirements. The binding layer adapts it to the
   selected renderer.
7. Produce a durable workflow specification that distinguishes researched evidence, creative
   inference, and unavailable live discovery.

## Boundaries

- Do not store, request, print, or publish provider secrets.
- Do not use for final UI implementation, API-key storage, or provider-specific secret management.
- Do not bypass a missing money gate with direct HTTP, a helper script, or ambient credentials.
- Do not use for product-shot, screen-recording, or multi-actor ad workflows.
- Do not invent creator examples, performance results, or provider completion state.

## Verification

- Company facts remain traceable to supplied business truth.
- The concept preserves a single person, selfie framing, believable action, and dialogue continuity.
- The reference asset is real and reachable through the declared capability.
- A blocked discovery remains blocked while the prompt and payload method can still be completed.

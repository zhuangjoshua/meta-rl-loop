# Distribution

Own campaign strategy and cross-channel coordination: the audience, offer, lane selection, sequence,
creative needs, and learning loop. Channel-native execution stays with the channel-specific method.
Use it when the business needs outbound demand creation or campaign iteration, or when the operator
asks to launch, continue, or review outreach or campaigns.

## Method

1. Read current business, market evidence, product truth, campaign history, and recent metrics.
2. State the campaign objective, target segment, offer, proof, call to action, and success signal.
3. Select the smallest set of channels that can test the hypothesis; explain why each belongs.
4. Define one coherent campaign with channel-specific adaptations rather than unrelated posts.
5. Identify required creative and route it to the appropriate creative method before execution.
6. Draft testable artifacts and a bounded sequence with ownership, timing, stop conditions, and the
   next measurement checkpoint.
7. Use only bound execution capabilities for real external actions. A local draft is not a send.
8. On continuation, compare actual response with the hypothesis and change one material variable at
   a time.

## Boundaries

- X posts, replies, and thread handling belong to `takyon-x`.
- Channel-owned Reddit execution, including organic posting or comments and paid Reddit ad launch,
  control, or metrics work, belongs to its channel method.
- Live paid Meta ad execution belongs to its channel method.
- Product-surface changes belong to `takyon-product`.
- Never claim an outreach action, post, or job happened without authoritative completion evidence.

## Verification

- Every lane serves the same audience, offer, and measurable hypothesis.
- Real actions are distinguished from drafts and suppressed tests.
- Campaign continuation cites observed results rather than generic best practices.
- Missing authority or channel support remains an explicit blocker.
